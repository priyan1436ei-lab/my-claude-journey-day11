# Day 11 – ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator

## Objective

Learn how to use AI to analyze a resume against a target Job Description (JD), identify ATS gaps, and generate an optimized resume for improved recruiter visibility.

## Key Learnings

* ATS systems prioritize keyword matching and resume structure.
* Professional summaries should align with the target role.
* Skills should be organized and relevant to the job description.
* Resume formatting impacts ATS readability.
* AI can significantly improve resume optimization while maintaining factual accuracy.
* Gap analysis helps identify missing skills and keywords required by employers.
